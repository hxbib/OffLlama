What this means for us:
- A Reflex student trained on Spark Open labels is a **Derivative**. So we must (1) include the copyright notice, this license and the Usage Policy (Exhibit A) with the student/app, and (2) show "Built with Muse Spark Open" in the app UI (Settings → About) or docs.

- The Usage Policy (Exhibit A) also applies to Derivatives, e.g. it bans "Representing that the use of Muse Spark Open or outputs are human generated".

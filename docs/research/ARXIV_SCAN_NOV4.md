# Link scan for the last hours and the roadmap (Nov 4 2026, ~6 AM ET)

Each item was opened on Nov 4 (arXiv abstract page, GitHub API + README, HF API + card, or the page itself). Verdicts:
**use-now** = usable today with no training and no spend (in practice, writing and citations); **roadmap** = a credible next step; **n/a** = not applicable.
Standing constraints apply: only Meta models ship, no `trust_remote_code`, no training or credit spend without Habib's yes.

## Top 3 use-now (all doc-only, $0)
1. **Cite Meta's own lineage: Spark → Glimmer → Reflex.** Meta's blog says Glimmer was pre-trained "on Muse Spark's outputs using logit distillation", and the tech report is now on arXiv (2610.24628). Our student is distilled from Glimmer's letter logits, so this is the cleanest "Use of Meta AI tooling" sentence available. (Used in JUDGE_BRIEF and RELATED_WORK.)
2. **Name the closest prior art and the difference.** Uddin & Bauer (ACL 2026 SRW) already certify a routing threshold with Clopper–Pearson, essentially our bound for cloud model pairs. Conformal Cascade (2607.25018) and CR² (2605.12001) are already cited in `pg_train/conformal.py`. SCOPE (ICML 2026) and CopRoute (NeurIPS 2026 On-Device Intelligence workshop) turned up in the search below. Our difference: the score is the student's own letter softmax, LTT bounds the decisions the phone actually executes, and escalation is also shaped by offline operation and a privacy veto. Saying this helps Technical Depth and keeps the "no prior art" claim honest.
3. **Explain the Q2_K failure with published evidence.** Our post-training Q2_K student collapsed (cal acc 0.66, bound 30.4%, QUANT_IDENTITY.md). ParetoQ (Meta, NeurIPS 2025) and Apple's 2-bit on-device model both reach 2-bit only with quantization-aware training. That is one line for the negative-results section, and the 30/60/90 plan has the QAT follow-up.

## Two searches
**"arxiv 2026 on-device LLM selective prediction conformal routing".** New and relevant items. These are related work, not things to use now:
- **CopRoute** (arXiv:2610.13276; NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on On-Device Intelligence, ODI 2026): routing over model sets that accounts for correlated failures. *That workshop is the natural venue for a Pocket Glimmer write-up.*
- **SCOPE** (arXiv:2602.13110, ICML 2026): selective conformal LLM judging with an order-swapped uncertainty score. This is direct support for our permutation-averaged readout under conformal control.
- **UCCI** (arXiv:2605.18796): isotonic calibration of token-margin uncertainty for cascade routing. Isotonic regression on 180 rows is risky. Revisit when the calibration set is bigger.
- **Selective Deferred Routing** (ICML 2026 poster): local SLM plus a learned deferral module that picks a remote LLM.
- **Conformal LLM Routing with Distribution-Free Safety Guarantees** (Uddin & Bauer, ACL 2026 SRW): a Clopper–Pearson-calibrated threshold on a logistic gate bounds the cheap model's failure rate among routed queries. **The closest mechanism to ours.** Cite it, and do not claim certified routing thresholds as new.
- Threshold-Aware Conformal Routing (2610.02487) is about simulation surrogates: **n/a**.

**"Muse Glimmer quantization iPhone".** No source shows Glimmer running on an iPhone. Unsloth's guide lists 2-bit at 12–14 GB RAM/VRAM and "runs locally on 18GB" setups. The model card's smallest official build (K-Quant-17GB) targets 24 GB. A Kaitchup review (paywalled beyond the intro) says Glimmer "tolerates quantization very well" down to ~5.4 bpw. The official tech report is arXiv:2610.24628 (Oct 2026). This matches our measurement (IQ2_M 9.85 GB; Q1_0 incoherent) and our choice to keep Glimmer as teacher and judge.
